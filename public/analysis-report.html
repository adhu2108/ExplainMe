Clause 8.2: Limitation of Liability Cap
Critical Risk
"Nothing in this Agreement shall limit or exclude either party's liability for breaches of confidentiality or indemnification obligations under Clause 9, which shall remain entirely uncapped."
AI Compliance Analysis

The clause establishes uncapped liability for general breaches of confidentiality and indemnity, which exposes your business to unbounded financial exposure in the event of accidental leaks.

Recommendation: Propose a reciprocal liability cap (e.g. limiting liability to $250,000 or 2x fees paid) to isolate operational risk.
Clause 11.4: Exclusivity Obligations
Warning Risk
"For a period of twelve (12) months following the termination of this Agreement, the receiving party agrees not to engage, solicit, or enter into similar evaluations with competitors listed in Exhibit B."
AI Compliance Analysis

The 12-month post-termination non-solicit and exclusivity period is overly restrictive and may inhibit your engineering team from working with other market players.

Recommendation: Reduce non-solicit term to 6 months and limit non-solicit exclusively to executive-level hires.
